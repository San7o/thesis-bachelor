\chapter{Linux Tracing}
\label{cha:linux-tracing}

The Linux tracing infrastructure has greately evolved over the years, and
continues to evolve rapidly driven mostly by an interest in low-overhead kernel
observability from cloud providers and hyperscalers. There has been a shift from
static tracing to dynamic tracing, to a fully programmable ecosystem.

In this section we discuss various solutions the Linux kernel provides for
tracing and monitoring.
